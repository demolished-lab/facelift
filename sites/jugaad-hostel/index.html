06 Stay FAQ — What guests ask before they book

Short answers, no surprises.

What time is check-in? 24hr front desk?

Front desk is 24 hours (Facts.hours). Check-in/out windows vary by room — confirm with host when you inquire. Whatever time your train lands, someone is at the desk.

confirm with host

Visitors & house rules?

Shared dorms are for registered guests only. Private rooms — ask desk about daytime visitors. Quiet hours after 10pm, keep common spaces tidy.

confirm with host

Luggage storage — early arrival or late flight?

Yes — luggage storage is listed as included. Drop bags before check-in or after check-out; desk will hold them. Lockers in dorms — bring a small padlock.

confirm with host

Parking & cab service?

Both are offered as paid services via front desk. Street parking limited — reserve ahead if you drive. Cab can be arranged, fare confirmed before you ride.
