◆ Before you ask

Frequently asked

How do I start the admission process?

Open the enquiry composer on the contact page, pick Admissions, add class and subject if relevant, then copy or send. We reply with the next step.

What programmes do you run?

The grid above fills verbatim once the owner publishes it. Until then the fastest way is to ask directly — the composer routes your question intact.

How do I ask about fees?

Choose Fees & batch details. Fees are confirmed by the complex directly — no number is guessed on-page.

What are your timings?

Timings appear on the contact card once published; the composer also carries a timings question straight to us.

Do I need an account or app?

No account, no app, no form marathon. One message does it.
